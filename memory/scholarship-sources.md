## Daily Brief Rules
- Section 1: Best-fit scholarships for Tony.
  - Find the best tech/STEM-friendly or high-GPA/junior-friendly scholarships.
  - Prefer NY/NYC and legitimate merit/need/hybrid aid.
  - Verify deadline, eligibility, amount, sponsor/original link, and account/login requirement.
  - For each scholarship, include a compact application plan: what Tony needs to have, how to apply, what to prepare first, and the next 1–3 concrete steps to maximize his chance.
- Section 2: NYC-area tech/STEM programs, courses, internships, or pre-college opportunities.
  - Focus on NYC universities/colleges and nearby institutions only unless Tony asks wider.
  - Prefer programs that build coding, software, engineering, research, project, or job-ready skills.
  - Rank by real skill gain, CV value, institution quality, eligibility for high-school juniors/seniors, deadline, and cost/value.
- Keep outputs concise to save model credits: shortlist only the best 2–4 total items daily, not exhaustive scans.
- Rotate sources across days instead of checking every source deeply every day.
- Never create accounts, submit applications/forms, email anyone, or share personal data without Tony's explicit approval.
- Log strong opportunities in `memory/opportunities.md` and deadline-heavy items in `memory/deadlines.md` when directly researching, but daily cron briefs should avoid unnecessary file writes unless a high-value deadline is found.
